# DC and nearby speakeasies, ranked by entrance secrecy

Researched November 8, 2026. The list contains 15 venues in Washington, DC, plus Captain Gregory’s in nearby Alexandria, Virginia.

**For the strongest hidden-entry experience, start with The Mirror or Out of Office at Manifest.**

- **Secrecy /10:** my assessment of how difficult the entrance is to decipher. 9 means a disguised doorway; 7–8 means an inconspicuous entrance or an entry ritual; 5–6 means a less obvious route; 2–3 means straightforward hotel-bar access. Waits and popularity do not affect this score.
- **Popularity /5:** a consistent proxy based on the linked Google review count: 1 = under 50; 2 = 50–199; 3 = 200–499; 4 = 500–999; 5 = 1,000+. This measures online visibility, not tonight's crowd or booking difficulty.
- **Online ratings:** Google scores reproduced by the linked public listings, except Sidecar’s explicitly marked historical OpenTable score. These are indexed snapshots, not a live Google feed; mirrors can disagree.

All addresses below are in Washington, DC, except the explicitly marked Alexandria venue. Rows with equal secrecy scores are tied.
