    A guide to the guide

    Secrecy / 10 rates how hard the entrance is to decipher. It does not measure popularity or waiting time.

    Popularity / 5 is a review-volume proxy: 1 = under 50 reviews, 2 = 50–199, 3 = 200–499, 4 = 500–999, 5 = 1,000+. Sidecar has no reliable current score.

    Online ratings are indexed Google snapshots, except Sidecar’s historical OpenTable score. Manifest, Chicken + Whiskey, and Doi Moi use shared business ratings. Capo has a small, inconsistent review sample. Check the linked sources before a visit.

    Pins are approximate street-address locations from the US Census Geocoder, not exact doorway locations. Use the entry notes for concealed, side-street, upstairs, or alley entrances.

    Export pins saves the included bars as a KML file you can import into Google My Maps. No account is needed to use this map; an internet connection is needed for the street tiles.
